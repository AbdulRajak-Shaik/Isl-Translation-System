# Research References and Academic Bibliography

This document provides formal citations and persistent identifiers (DOIs / URLs) for all research papers, datasets, frameworks, and benchmarks utilized in this project.

---

## 1. Continuous Indian Sign Language Translation & Benchmarks

1. **ISLTranslate (ACL 2023):**
   - *Citation:* Joshi, A., Agrawal, S., & Modi, A. (2023). "ISLTranslate: Dataset for Translating Indian Sign Language." In *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2023*, pages 10515–10528, Toronto, Canada. Association for Computational Linguistics.
   - *URL:* [https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.665/](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.665/)
   - *Code & Data:* [https://github.com/Exploration-Lab/ISLTranslate](https://github.com/Exploration-Lab/ISLTranslate)

2. **iSign Benchmark (ACL 2024):**
   - *Citation:* Joshi, A., et al. (2024). "iSign: Benchmark for Indian Sign Language." In *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2024*, pages 10078–10098, Bangkok, Thailand. Association for Computational Linguistics.
   - *URL:* [https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.643/](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.643/)
   - *Project Page:* [https://exploration-lab.github.io/iSign/](https://exploration-lab.github.io/iSign/)

3. **ISL-CSLTR Continuous Corpus (Mendeley Data 2021):**
   - *Citation:* Elakkiya, R., & Natarajan, B. (2021). "ISL-CSLTR: Indian Sign Language Dataset for Continuous Sign Language Translation and Recognition." *Mendeley Data*, V1.
   - *DOI:* `10.17632/kcmpdxky7p.1`
   - *URL:* [https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/kcmpdxky7p/1](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/kcmpdxky7p/1)

4. **Continuous Translation Broadcast Corpus (Nature Scientific Reports):**
   - *Citation:* "Indian Sign Language News Translation: A Continuous Translation Corpus from Broadcast Media." *Scientific Reports*, Nature Publishing Group.
   - *DOI:* `10.1038/s41598-026-60893-0`
   - *URL:* [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-60893-0](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-60893-0)

## 2. Isolated Sign Recognition & Auxiliary Learning

5. **INCLUDE Dataset (ACM Multimedia 2020):**
   - *Citation:* Sridhar, P., et al. (2020). "INCLUDE: A Large Scale Dataset for Indian Sign Language Recognition." In *Proceedings of the 28th ACM International Conference on Multimedia (MM '20)*, pages 1310–1318.
   - *DOI:* `10.1145/3394171.3413528`
   - *Zenodo Archive:* [https://zenodo.org/record/4010759](https://zenodo.org/record/4010759)

6. **CISLR (EMNLP / Exploration-Lab):**
   - *Citation:* Joshi, A., Bhat, A., & Modi, A. "CISLR: Corpus for Indian Sign Language Recognition." *Hugging Face Datasets*, Exploration-Lab.
   - *URL:* [https://github.com/Exploration-Lab/CISLR](https://github.com/Exploration-Lab/CISLR)

7. **Official ISLRTC Sign Dictionary:**
   - *Citation:* Indian Sign Language Research and Training Centre (ISLRTC). "Indian Sign Language Dictionary (10,000 Signs)." Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.
   - *URL:* [https://islrtc.nic.in/](https://islrtc.nic.in/) and [https://data.gov.in/catalog/indian-sign-language-dictionary](https://data.gov.in/catalog/indian-sign-language-dictionary)

## 3. Indic Machine Translation and Neural Speech Synthesis

8. **AI4Bharat IndicTrans2:**
   - *Citation:* Gala, J., et al. (2023). "IndicTrans2: Towards High-Quality and Accessible Machine Translation Models for all 22 Scheduled Indian Languages." *arXiv preprint arXiv:2305.16307*.
   - *GitHub:* [https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicTrans2)
   - *Model Checkpoints:* `ai4bharat/indictrans2-en-indic-1B` and `ai4bharat/indictrans2-en-indic-dist-200M` on Hugging Face.

9. **Sign Language Transformers:**
   - *Citation:* Camgoz, N. C., et al. (2020). "Sign Language Transformers: Joint End-to-end Sign Language Recognition and Translation." In *IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, pages 10023–10033.
   - *DOI:* `10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.01004`
