# Known Limitations and Failure Mode Analysis

**System:** Continuous Indian Sign Language Translation to English & Telugu  
**Status:** Transparent Engineering Disclosure  

In adherence to scientific integrity principles, this document outlines the verified limitations, boundary conditions, and known failure modes of the system.

---

## 1. Computer Vision & Landmark Extraction Limitations

1. **Severe Motion Blur and Fast Signing:**
   - Standard 24–30 FPS webcams often produce motion blur during rapid hand transitions. When fingers blend into background pixels, skeletal landmark confidence drops significantly.
   - *Mitigation:* The system computes confidence per keypoint and interpolates across adjacent frames; if mean hand visibility drops below $0.4$, the frame is flagged as degraded.

2. **Self-Occlusion and Depth Ambiguity:**
   - When one hand occludes the other (two-handed signs where hands cross) or when hands touch the face/torso, 2D-to-3D landmark estimators can flip left/right hand identity or collapse 3D depth.
   - *Mitigation:* The temporal encoder applies self-attention over extended windows ($T \ge 60$ frames) so that context before and after occlusion clarifies the sign.

3. **Lighting & Complex Backgrounds:**
   - Low-light environments and severe backlighting degrade optical contrast. While coordinate normalization minimizes background dependence, landmark detection still requires adequate illumination.

---

## 2. Linguistic & Regional Dialect Variations

1. **Regional Dialect Diversity in ISL:**
   - Indian Sign Language possesses regional lexical variations across Northern (Delhi, UP), Western (Maharashtra), and Southern (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh/Telangana) signing communities.
   - Most academic datasets (such as ISLTranslate, iSign, and ISLRTC) reflect standardized Northern/Central ISL varieties.
   - *Limitation:* Signers using localized colloquial signs from non-standard regional communities may encounter lower recognition accuracy.

2. **Fingerspelling & Out-of-Vocabulary (OOV) Proper Nouns:**
   - Complex names of individuals, rare medical terms, or obscure place names fingerspelled in ISL two-handed alphabet may produce character-level transcription errors if the character duration is under 3 frames.

---

## 3. Downstream Machine Translation Cascade

1. **Error Compounding in English → Telugu:**
   - Because Telugu translation is generated by cascading the ISL-to-English output through IndicTrans2, any syntactic omission or grammatical distortion in the English sentence will propagate into Telugu.
   - *Mitigation:* An uncertainty detector inspects the decoder's sequence log-likelihood. If the English translation has high uncertainty, downstream Telugu translation is flagged with an explicit advisory tag (`[Uncertain Prediction / Needs Verification]`).

---
